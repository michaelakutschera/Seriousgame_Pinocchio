About this game

What is it about?

The game is about how media shape stories and meaning. The chapters 17 and 18 from Le avventure di Pinocchio.
Storia di un burattino (1883) from Carlo Collodi are told
in the English translation, in form of a book, a comic, an audiobook and social media.

Why does it matter?

Every story is brought to life through a medium, which in turn shapes our perception of it. Each medium has its own specific characteristics, and forces the story to change and adapt.
The game therefore asks: What remains the same, and what changes? How does the medium influence our perception?

Controls & Rules

Start & Stop

Start begins a level.
Stop pauses everything, so you can take a break without losing your place.

Back to Start

Returns you to this page at any time. Once a level is completed it is locked — each chapter can only be played once.
It is possible to "Reset Progress" and start with level 1.

After each level

Every level ends with a short evaluation.
These questions are not about what happened in the chapters.
It is about how the medium itself told it.

Reward System

Collecting Humanity

Every level rewards you with Humanity Points: for correctly completing the chapter under time pressure, and for thinking carefully about how each medium tells it.
Your total Humanity Points carry over from level to level, and you can see them at the top of every page.

The Ending

After Level 4, Pinocchio's fate depends on how much Humanity Points
you've collected across all four levels.
Collect enough, and the Fairy with the blue hair will turn him into a real boy.
Fall short – by running out of time or misreading how a medium works – and Pinocchio remains a wooden puppet.
