Pinocchio's most famous moment — the chapter about lying and his growing nose — is told four times in four different media: a book, a comic, a film, and a video game. Play through all four, collect Humanity, and decide Pinocchio's fate.

About this game

What is it about?

It is about how media shape stories — not just carry them. The same scene from Le avventure di Pinocchio — Storia di un burattino (Carlo Collodi, 1883) is retold in a book, a comic, a film, and a video game.

Why does it matter?

By comparing the same content across media, the game asks: why is one version funnier, darker, or more detailed than another? What stays the same about Pinocchio across more than a century of adaptations — and what changes, and why?

How to Play

Controls & Rules

Timer

Each level has a timer in the top bar, counting how long you spend on that chapter. It starts when you press Start and pauses when you press Stop.

Start & Stop

Start begins a level and its timer. Stop pauses everything, so you can take a break without losing your place.

Back to Start

Returns you to this page at any time. Once a level is completed it is locked — each chapter can only be played through once.

After each level

Every level ends with a short evaluation on its own page. These questions are not about what happened in the story — they ask how the medium itself told it: through pictures, panels, camera shots, or interaction.

The Reward System

Will Pinocchio become a real boy?

Collecting Humanity

Every level rewards you with Humanity points: for correctly completing the chapter under time pressure, and for thinking carefully about how each medium tells it. Your total carries over from level to level — you can see it at the top of every page.

The Ending

After Level 4, Pinocchio's fate depends on how much Humanity you've collected across all four levels. Collect enough, and the Fairy will turn him into a real boy. Fall short — for example, by running out of time, choosing the wrong sentence, or misreading how a medium works — and Pinocchio remains a wooden puppet.

Pinocchio: One Story, Many Media — a serious game prototype for a university media studies project (4 ECTS). Story text adapted from Le avventure di Pinocchio — Storia di un burattino by Carlo Collodi (1883); illustrations in Level 1 after Enrico Mazzanti's original 1883 drawings.
