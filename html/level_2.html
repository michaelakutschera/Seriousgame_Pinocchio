Level 2 – The Comic

This chapter is told across twelve comic panels.
The panels are shuffled out of order.
Tap two to swap them and arrange the pages in correct reading order.
A panel that belongs on Page 1 might start out on Page 2.

There's a minimum number of swaps needed to fix it. The closer you get to the minimum, the more Humanity you earn.
A short evaluation follows at the end of the level.
