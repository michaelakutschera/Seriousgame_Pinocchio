Level 2 — The Comic

This chapter is told across twelve comic panels, six per page. When the comic opens, all twelve panels are shuffled together – a panel that belongs on page 1 might start out on page 2, and vice versa.

Tap one panel, then tap another to swap their places. Arrange the whole spread into the correct reading order.

Gaining Humanity is not just about finishing: it's about how directly you get there. The whole puzzle starts with a best possible number of swaps needed to fix the shuffle. The closer your actual number of swaps is to that minimum, the more Humanity you earn.

A short evaluation on a separate page follows at the end.
